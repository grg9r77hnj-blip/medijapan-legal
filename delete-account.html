MediJapan — Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your MediJapan account (app ID com.medijapan.medijapan_ai, published by MediJapan) and what happens to your data when you do.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open the MediJapan app.
  2. Tap the person icon on the Home screen.
  3. Go to Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

If you can no longer access the app, email medijapanapp@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What we keep, and why

  • Anonymous medicine catalog entries. If a product you scanned was added to our medicine catalog, the product information stays, because it describes a medicine and not you. The link to your account is removed, so it cannot be traced back to you.
  • Purchase records held by the app store. Subscriptions are billed by the app store you installed the App from, not by us. Deleting your MediJapan account does not cancel a subscription and does not erase the store's own billing records. Cancel a subscription in that store's account settings, and contact the store about its records.
  • Backups. Deleted data may remain briefly in encrypted backups before those backups are rotated and overwritten.

Photos you scan

Photos of medicine packages are sent to our AI service to be read and are never stored on our servers. Any copy on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
