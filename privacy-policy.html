Privacy Policy — MediJapan AI

Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]. This app is provided by [LEGAL NAME / OWNER] ("we", "us"). Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

MediJapan AI helps foreigners in Japan understand over-the-counter (OTC) medicines. It provides information only — it does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your email address (used to sign in).
  • Health profile (optional, you provide it): date of birth, whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding, allergies, and any health conditions you enter. This is used to tailor OTC information and warnings to you.
  • Symptom chat: the messages you send to the in-app assistant.
  • Scans: text and/or photos of medicine packages you scan, and the resulting translation/warnings.
  • Location: your device location, only when you open the pharmacy finder, to show nearby pharmacies. It is used to run the search and is not used to track you.
  • Usage & purchase data: counts of daily AI/scan usage (to apply free limits) and subscription status (if you subscribe).
  • Technical: basic device and diagnostic information needed to run the app.

We do not ask for or intentionally collect your name, precise address, government IDs, or payment card numbers (purchases are handled by Apple/Google).

2. How your health data is treated

Your health profile, symptom messages, and scans can reveal information about your health, which is sensitive under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) and similar laws. We collect it only with your action of entering or scanning it, use it only to provide the app's features, and never sell it.

3. AI processing

To answer symptom questions and interpret scans, the relevant text and images are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic, the Claude API) for processing and returned to you. This processing is necessary to provide the feature you requested.

4. Service providers we use

  • Supabase — secure database, authentication, and storage (backend).
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI processing of symptom chat and scans.
  • Google Maps Platform — displaying the pharmacy map.
  • RevenueCat and Apple / Google — subscriptions and billing (if you subscribe).
  • [If Firebase/analytics is enabled at launch, list it here.]

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms. We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep it & your controls

We keep your data while your account is active. You can:

  • Delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete my account). This permanently removes your profile, chats, scans, favorites, reminders, and usage data.
  • Edit or remove individual items (health profile, favorites, reminders, history) in the app.
  • Contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] to request access or deletion.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls that restrict each user's data to that user. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Children

MediJapan AI is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The medicine information is general OTC guidance for adults; always follow package labels and consult a pharmacist for children.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data on servers outside your country (for example, in the United States). By using the app you understand your data may be processed in those locations.

9. Not medical advice

MediJapan AI provides information to help you choose OTC products and communicate with a pharmacist. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. In an emergency, call 119 in Japan.
