MediJapan — Support

MediJapan helps foreigners in Japan understand over-the-counter (OTC) medicines: scanning packages, translating labels, checking symptoms against common OTC products, and finding nearby pharmacies. It provides information only — it does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe.

Contact us

For support, feedback, or account requests (including data deletion), email medijapanapp@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Open the app → tap the person icon on the Home screen → Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, chats, scans, favorites, reminders, and usage data.
  • Is this medical advice? No. MediJapan gives general information about OTC products. Always read the package, follow its instructions, and ask a pharmacist — especially if pregnant, breastfeeding, buying for a child, or taking other medicines. In an emergency, call 119 in Japan.
  • Where does the medicine information come from? Official Japanese package inserts and public regulatory sources (KEGG MEDICUS / JAPIC / PMDA).
